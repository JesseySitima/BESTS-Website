Spirometry Testing

Measures lung function and provides accurate test results for healthcare professionals to effectively diagnose and monitor respiratory issues, including:

  • Restrictive impairment — e.g., lung fibrosis, pneumothorax
  • Obstructive impairment — e.g., Asthma, COPD
  • Mixed impairment — combination of restrictive and obstructive patterns
