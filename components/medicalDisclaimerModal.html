Privacy Policy — BESTS Lung Function Experts

Last updated: 13 June 2026

1. Introduction

BESTS Lung Function Experts (“BESTS”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Malawi’s Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act 2016.

BESTS is a technical lung function testing service that abides to Good Clinical Practices (GCP), ATS/ERS 2022 guidelines and international standards. We do not provide medical diagnosis. Test results must be interpreted by your licensed healthcare provider.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide testing services:

  • Contact data: Name, phone number, email, WhatsApp handle. Used for appointments and sending results.
  • Referral data: Referring doctor's name, clinic, referral letter. Used to coordinate care and share results.
  • Health data: Age, height, weight, smoking history, symptoms, contraindications, and spirometry results.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, cookies and website usage.
  • Communication data: WhatsApp messages, call logs and emails.

We do not collect financial card data on this website.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide spirometry and lung function testing
  • Send results to you and/or your referring healthcare provider
  • Manage appointments, reminders and follow-ups
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell your data.

4. WhatsApp & Third-Party Platforms

If you contact or book via WhatsApp, Meta Platforms Inc. will also process your data under WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy. We cannot control Meta’s data practices.

5. Data Sharing

  • With your referring doctor or clinic for clinical interpretation
  • With secure service providers under confidentiality agreements
  • If required by Malawi law

6. Data Security

We use password-protected systems, secure storage and limited staff access to protect your information.

7. Data Retention

  • Appointment data: 2 years
  • Health/test data: 7 years

8. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion subject to legal requirements
  • Withdraw communication consent
